Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and payment details. We also collect transaction data through our integration with Payuee.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process orders, facilitate escrow payments, arrange delivery, and provide customer support. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Payment Security

All payment processing is handled by Payuee. GadgetHub does not store your bank account or card details. Payuee uses 256-bit AES encryption and HMAC-SHA256 signing for all transactions.

4. Cookies

We use HttpOnly cookies for authentication. These cookies cannot be accessed by client-side scripts and are transmitted securely over HTTPS.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request account deletion by contacting support.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Contact us at support@gadgethub.ng to exercise these rights.

7. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, email us at privacy@gadgethub.ng.
