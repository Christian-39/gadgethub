Returns & Refunds

Return Policy

Due to the escrow nature of our marketplace, returns are handled differently than traditional e-commerce. Funds remain locked until you confirm delivery, giving you full control.

Refund Eligibility

  • Item not delivered within estimated timeline
  • Item significantly different from description
  • Damaged or defective products
  • Wrong item delivered
  • Order cancelled within the 30% delivery window

How Refunds Work

When you report an issue or cancel an eligible order, Payuee investigates and processes the refund directly to your wallet. You do not need to return physical items for escrow-protected orders — the funds were never released to the vendor.

Timeline

Refunds are typically processed within 24-48 hours of approval. Wallet credits are instant once approved.

Non-Refundable Items

Orders where delivery has been confirmed via QR scan and transaction PIN cannot be refunded unless fraud is proven. Always inspect your delivery before confirming.
